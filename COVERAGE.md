# Coverage and methodology

The service searches a curated research collection, not the live web. Transaction records were consolidated from supplied monthly reports, with duplicates and related financing/acquisition milestones reconciled in the dataset. Event candidates were consolidated from supplied research. Field-level sources, review status and unresolved conflicts are retained.

- Transactions cover January–September 2026.
- Event research covers October 2026–December 2027.
- Review labels do not mean every field has been freshly independently verified.
- Pending candidates and supplied_unverified events must not be presented as confirmed facts.
- Do not silently convert currencies or equate undisclosed amounts with zero.
- A round extension can be part of an existing round; an acquisition announcement and completion can be milestones of the same deal.
- Conference venue, dates, attendance format and registration status can differ across supplied sources. Confirm with the organizer.
- No exhaustive market coverage or automatic update schedule is promised.

Functional tests compare behavior against the curated collection. The supplied Claude CSV records 27 PASS results for EV-04–EV-30, with earlier cases supplied separately. This is not an independently audited consolidated 30-case run or fresh factual verification of the full collection.
